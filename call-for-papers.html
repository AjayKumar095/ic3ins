Call for Papers

The 1st International Conference on Computing, Communication, Intelligence & Networked Systems (IC3INS-2027) invites original research contributions addressing the latest developments in computing, communication, intelligence, artificial intelligence, intelligent systems, IoT, edge computing, intelligent perception, and emerging networked technologies. The conference provides a global platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, practitioners, and scholars to exchange ideas, present innovative research, discuss emerging challenges, and explore transformative solutions in intelligent and connected technologies.

Authors are invited to submit full papers presenting original, previously unpublished research that is not currently under review or consideration by any other conference or journal. Submitted papers should be prepared in the IEEE double-column format and should not exceed six pages, including figures, tables, results, and references. All accepted and presented papers will be considered for submission to IEEE Xplore for possible inclusion in its digital library (subject to approval).

Conference Scope

IC3INS-2027 invites original and high-quality research contributions from researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, developers, and practitioners worldwide. The conference aims to explore recent advancements, innovative methodologies, and real-world applications in computing, communication, intelligence, artificial intelligence, intelligent networked systems, and emerging computing technologies.

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Intelligent Computing
  • Neural Networks, Deep Learning, and Reinforcement Learning
  • Natural Language Processing, Expert Systems, and Intelligent Agents
  • Fuzzy Logic, Evolutionary Computation, and Soft Computing
  • Intelligent Systems and Networked Intelligence
  • Internet of Things (IoT), Industrial IoT, and Smart Connected Systems
  • Edge, Fog, and Cloud Computing
  • AI-Enabled Communication and Network Optimization
  • Intelligent Wireless Networks and 5G/6G Technologies
  • Software-Defined Networking and Next-Generation Communication Systems
  • Intelligent Vision, Computer Vision, and Image and Video Analytics
  • Medical Imaging, Remote Sensing, and Smart Perception
  • Object Detection, Scene Understanding, and Autonomous Surveillance
  • Cyber-Physical Systems, Digital Twins, and Smart Infrastructure
  • Big Data, Intelligent Analytics, and Data-Driven Computing
  • Smart Cities, Sustainable Computing, and Emerging Intelligent Applications
  • Intelligent Healthcare, Diagnostics, and Healthcare Technologies
  • Blockchain, Distributed Systems, and Secure Intelligent Computing

Within these tracks, authors are encouraged to submit work spanning neural networks and deep learning, IoT and 5G/6G technologies, machine vision and cyber-physical systems, intelligent communication and networked systems, and AI-driven applications in medical, industrial, and urban infrastructure.
