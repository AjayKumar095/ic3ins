Call for Papers

The 1st International Conference on Computing, Communication, Intelligence & Networked Systems (IC3INS-2027) invites original research contributions addressing the latest developments in computing, communication, intelligence, artificial intelligence, intelligent systems, IoT, edge computing, intelligent perception, and emerging networked technologies. The conference provides a global platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, practitioners, and scholars to exchange ideas, present innovative research, discuss emerging challenges, and explore transformative solutions in intelligent and connected technologies.

Authors are invited to submit full papers presenting original, previously unpublished research that is not currently under review or consideration by any other conference or journal. Submitted papers should be prepared in the double-column format and should not exceed six pages, including figures, tables, results, and references. All accepted and presented papers will be considered for submission to for possible inclusion in its digital library (subject to approval).

Conference Scope

IC3INS-2027 invites original and high-quality research contributions from researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, developers, and practitioners worldwide. The conference aims to explore recent advancements, innovative methodologies, and real-world applications in computing, communication, intelligence, artificial intelligence, intelligent networked systems, and emerging computing technologies.

Within these thematic areas, authors are encouraged to submit original research contributions that advance intelligent computing, computational intelligence, communication technologies, networked systems, and autonomous applications. Particular emphasis is placed on the integration of AI and machine learning with connected and distributed infrastructures, intelligent perception, adaptive decision-making, and resilient cyber-physical systems, with the aim of enabling next-generation digital transformation in healthcare, industry, smart cities, and society. Submissions should demonstrate a clear technical contribution to one or more of the conference’s core themes.

Track Details

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to, the following areas:

  • Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Intelligent Computing
  • Neural Networks, Deep Learning, and Reinforcement Learning
  • Natural Language Processing, Generative AI, and Intelligent Agents
  • Fuzzy Logic, Evolutionary Computation, and Soft Computing
  • Explainable, Trustworthy, and Responsible AI
  • Intelligent Optimization, Predictive Analytics, and Knowledge Discovery
  • Big Data, Intelligent Analytics, and Data-Driven Computing
  • AI in Healthcare, Diagnostics, and Decision Support
  • Internet of Things (IoT), Industrial IoT, and Smart Connected Systems
  • Edge, Fog, and Cloud Computing
  • Intelligent Wireless Communications, 5G/6G, and Emerging Network Technologies
  • AI-Enabled Communication, Network Modeling, and Performance Optimization
  • Software-Defined Networking and Network Virtualization
  • Network Security, Privacy, and Intrusion Detection
  • Resilient and Energy-Efficient Communication Networks
  • Blockchain, Distributed Systems, and Secure Networked Computing
  • Computer Vision, Image Processing, and Visual Intelligence
  • Image and Video Analytics, Object Detection, and Scene Understanding
  • Medical Imaging, Remote Sensing, and Multimodal Perception
  • Robotics, Autonomous Systems, and Multi-Agent Intelligence
  • Cyber-Physical Systems and Digital Twins
  • Intelligent Control and Autonomous Decision-Making
  • Connected Autonomous Systems and Intelligent Transportation
  • Self-Organizing, Adaptive, and Resilient Systems
  • Intelligent Applications in Healthcare, Industry, Smart Cities, and Digital Infrastructure
