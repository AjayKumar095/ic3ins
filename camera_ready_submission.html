Camera Ready Submission

Authors are required to adhere to all general and mandatory submission guidelines specified for the final preparation and submission of their manuscripts for consideration in IEEE Xplore.

Instructions for Authors

Authors are required to follow all the instructions listed under General and Required category for final submission of their manuscript to IEEE Xplore.

General Instructions

  • Authors are required to remove header and footer (if any) in their final camera ready manuscript.
  • Do not put any page numbers in the camera ready manuscript.
  • Remove the titles like Prof., Dr., Mr., Ms., etc. before the author name(s).
  • Remove hyperlinks (if any) in the final version of the manuscript.
  • Format the paper using the IEEE standard template.
  • In case you are using the IEEE LaTeX template, use the following \documentclass code to change the paper size from Letter to A4:

Required Instructions

  • Camera Ready / Final Manuscript — on or before September 20, 2027.
  • IEEE eCopyright form, via email received from IEEE — on or before September 20, 2027.
  • eRegistration form, duly completed — on or before September 20, 2027.

1. Camera Ready Submission

Download the IEEE Camera Ready Template for IC3INS-2027.

Click here:- Word / LaTeX.

The paper length should not exceed six (6) pages, IEEE two (2) column format. You need to modify your paper exactly as per the format requirements specified in the Camera Ready Format.

2. IEEE Copyright Form Submission

  • Step 1: IC3INS-2027 supports the submission of the eCopyright form via IEEE eCopyright, failing which you will not be able to publish your final manuscript(s).
  • Step 2: Kindly answer all questions asked during submission of the eCopyright form.

3. eRegistration Process

At least one author must register on or before September 20, 2027 to include the paper in the conference.

  • Step 1: Before starting eRegistration, you are required to have a copy of the following documents in hand:
    • Copy of Student ID card / Faculty ID Card
    • Copy of professional membership (IEEE)
    • Copy of bank transaction receipt / screenshot of online payment done
  • Step 2: For starting the eRegistration process, please click: To be updated.
  • Step 3: Authors claiming a discount for IEEE membership, student status, or both, must submit the scanned/PDF copy of the proof at meenu.gupta@geetauniversity.edu.in on or before September 20, 2027.
