About IC3INS-2027

Welcome to IC3INS-2027: the 1st International Conference on Computing, Communication, Intelligence & Networked Systems, organised by the School of Computer Science and Engineering, on 12th–13th November 2027 at Geeta University, Campus – Panipat, Haryana, India.

IC3INS-2027 is a premier international event aimed at fostering innovation, collaboration and knowledge exchange in the fields of computing, communication, intelligent computing, artificial intelligence and networked technologies. The conference serves as a multidisciplinary platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals and scholars to present their latest findings, discuss emerging trends and explore future directions in intelligent computing, communication technologies and networked systems. In a world increasingly driven by automation, digital transformation and smart systems, IC3INS-2027 addresses the pressing need to integrate intelligent computing, advanced communication and connected technologies to solve complex, real-world problems.

The conference encourages a synergistic blend of theory and application, with an emphasis on impactful, interdisciplinary research that can drive advancements in areas such as healthcare, smart cities, cybersecurity, IoT, intelligent systems and autonomous technologies. IC3INS-2027 is committed to academic excellence and aims to promote international collaboration by connecting participants with global thought leaders, encouraging thought-provoking discussion, and contributing to the evolution of next-generation computing, communication and intelligent networked systems.

All the accepted and presented papers in the Conference will be submitted to IEEE Xplore for possible inclusion in its digital library. (Subject to Approval)

Conference Objectives

IC3INS-2027 aims to promote high-quality research and facilitate the exchange of innovative ideas, emerging trends, and technological advancements in computing, communication, intelligence, artificial intelligence, and networked systems. The conference seeks to create a dynamic platform for researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and innovators to address contemporary challenges and explore future opportunities in intelligent and connected technologies.

  • Fostering interdisciplinary collaboration across computing, communication, artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analytics, intelligent systems, IoT, edge computing, and emerging smart technologies.
  • Promoting cutting-edge research and innovation in intelligent, adaptive, scalable, secure, and sustainable computing, communication, and networked systems.
  • Bridging theoretical research with real-world applications through practical solutions and innovative implementations in areas such as smart cities, healthcare, industry, communication, and connected environments.
  • Encouraging knowledge exchange and global collaboration among researchers, academicians, industry experts, practitioners, and young scholars.
  • Exploring emerging challenges and future directions in next-generation computing, intelligent systems, autonomous technologies, intelligent perception, communication, and networked technologies.
  • Facilitating industry–academia interaction to develop solution-oriented research addressing current industrial, technological, and societal needs.

IC3INS-2027 aspires to serve as a premier global platform for exploring transformative research and emerging technologies in computing, communication, intelligence, artificial intelligence, and networked systems. The conference will feature keynote addresses, technical paper presentations, interactive sessions, workshops, and panel discussions led by distinguished researchers and industry experts.

We warmly invite researchers, academicians, scholars, innovators, and industry professionals from across the globe to participate in this exciting forum and contribute to advancing innovation, academic excellence, knowledge exchange, and international collaboration in intelligent computing, communication, and networked technologies.
