Submission Guidelines

Authors are invited to contribute to IC3INS-2027 by submitting original research papers, technical studies, survey papers, project-based research, and industrial case studies presenting significant advancements in computational intelligence, artificial intelligence, intelligent networked systems, and emerging computing technologies. Contributions addressing theoretical developments, innovative methodologies, practical implementations, and real-world applications are welcome. Topics of interest include, but are not limited to, the following tracks:

  • Track 1: Artificial Intelligence & Computational Intelligence
  • Track 2: Intelligent Networked Systems, IoT & Edge Computing
  • Track 3: Intelligent Vision, Perception & Multimedia Analytics
  • Track 4: Emerging Intelligent Computing & Smart Technologies

Interested authors are invited to submit full-length papers presenting original and previously unpublished research, including relevant results, figures, tables, and references. All submissions must follow the prescribed IEEE conference paper format and submission guidelines.

Papers will be accepted only through electronic submission via Microsoft CMT using the official submission link provided below.

Plagiarism and AI-Generated Content Policy

All manuscripts submitted to IC3INS-2027 will undergo a double-blind peer-review process. Authors are therefore required to ensure that their identities, affiliations, acknowledgements, and other identifying information are not included in the initial manuscript submission.

IC3INS-2027 follows a strict policy against plagiarism and considers plagiarism and other forms of academic misconduct to be serious violations of research ethics. All submitted manuscripts will be screened for plagiarism and textual similarity. Manuscripts exceeding the permissible similarity threshold may be rejected without further review.

Only manuscripts with an initial similarity index below 15%, including references and DOI-related content, will be considered for further review. Authors are responsible for ensuring that their submissions are original and appropriately acknowledge all sources and prior work.

AI-Assisted and AI-Generated Content

Authors must ensure that the submitted manuscript represents their original scholarly work. The use of generative AI tools, including tools such as ChatGPT, to generate substantial portions of the manuscript, research content, analysis, results, or conclusions is not permitted unless explicitly allowed under the applicable IEEE policies and properly disclosed.

IC3INS-2027 reserves the right to conduct appropriate checks for plagiarism, manipulated content, and undisclosed AI-generated material at any stage of the publication process. If significant plagiarism, academic misconduct, or prohibited/undisclosed AI-generated content is identified at any stage, including after acceptance or registration, the manuscript may be rejected or withdrawn from publication, and registration fees, if already paid, may not be refundable.

Authors are solely responsible for the originality, accuracy, integrity, and ethical compliance of their submitted work.
